## Brand & Style

This design system blends the human-centric warmth and wanderlust of premier hospitality platforms with the crisp, deterministic speed of next-generation AI productivity suites. It establishes an atmosphere of precision, inspiration, and effortless clarity, optimized here for a light color mode experience.

The aesthetic fuses modern minimalism with precision-engineered translucent layering:
- **Calm Authority & Curiosity:** Interactions evoke the excitement of journey discovery while grounding complex algorithmic routing in reliable, structured surfaces.
- **Glass-Infused Precision:** Restrained glassmorphic layers convey depth without degrading data legibility. Translucent panels catch light cleanly against high-clarity canvas zones.
- **Hyper-Tactile Feedback:** Crisp edges, balanced border luminance, and micro-elevation keep high-density travel itineraries, flight matrices, and interactive map overlays clear and navigable.

## Colors

The palette balances soft pastel accents against structural muted tones within an immersive light mode environment:
- **Primary (`#EED3D9`):** Soft rose-pink representing forward movement, interactive waypoints, key action states, and generated AI recommendations.
- **Secondary (`#F5E8DD`):** Warm cream anchors typography, navigational rails, high-contrast badges, and primary structure.
- **Tertiary (`#CCD3CA`):** Muted sage green captures natural accents, rating milestones, priority alerts, and destination markers.
- **Neutral (`#B5C0D0` & `#FFFFFF`):** Multi-tiered cool slate-gray canvases tailored for light mode that reduce eye fatigue while providing high contrast for imagery and layered interactive glass sheets.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy features a unified single-font system powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all headings, body copy, and operational labels.

- **Headlines & Display:** Powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, tightly tracked headings deliver an editorial yet technical appearance, pairing well with visual travel imagery and dynamic data cards.
- **Body & Data Structures:** Powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, standard tracking with tall x-height optimizes scanning across complex multiline itineraries, airport transit timings, and booking notes.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, uppercase tracking on micro-labels (`label-sm`) injects high-density operational clarity reminiscent of flight boarding passes and analytical software.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a flexible 12-column dynamic grid with responsive gutters and strict proportional scaling:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max-container frame of 1440px. Supports 3-pane workflows: persistent conversational AI sidebar (360px), central itinerary schedule (flex), and dynamic live-map/media viewport.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters. Map and conversational assistant collapse into toggleable slide-over sheets or tabbed views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Content aligns in single-stream vertical cards with bottom-docked navigation and interactive sheets.
- **Rhythm:** Internal component spacing follows a base 4px/8px module, keeping dense schedules readable alongside large travel photography blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth relies on translucent surface glass, low-contrast structural borders, and oceanic ambient shadows tailored for light mode contexts:

- **Flat/Canvas (`Level 0`):** Default viewport background `#B5C0D0`. Zero elevation.
- **Base Glass (`Level 1`):** Surfaces use light-mode optimized translucent layering with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with a fine 1px border. Soft drop-shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(181, 192, 208, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Interactive (`Level 2`):** Cards on hover, itinerary pills, and segmented toolbars use high-opacity light surfaces with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered finely. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(181, 192, 208, 0.2), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(181, 192, 208, 0.08)`.
- **Modals & Overlays (`Level 3`):** Flight selectors, AI prompt sheets, and full itinerary drawers use solid light structural tones with deep ambient shadows (`0 20px 40px -8px rgba(237, 211, 217, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The system uses a balanced rounded geometry (Level 2):
- **Base Components:** Inputs, buttons, chips, and table cells carry `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- **Containers & Surfaces:** Destination cards, summary lists, and modal drawers scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for hero modules.
- **Floating Controls & Avatars:** Micro-controls, map markers, and quick-filter pills employ full-capsule styling (`9999px`) to maintain tactile contrast against structured itinerary cards.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* `#EED3D9` solid fill, dark slate text, 8px corner radius, with subtle top-highlight inset shadow. On hover: shifts to `#E0C2C8` with a low-opacity rose glow.
  - *Secondary / Glass:* Semi-translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), dark slate text (`#B5C0D0`), and subtle border.
  - *Ghost / Linear:* Borderless with transparent background, activating into soft slate (`rgba(181, 192, 208, 0.15)`) on pointer interaction.

- **Input Fields & AI Omnibar:**
  - Standard inputs: White surface, 1px `#B5C0D0` border, transitions to `#EED3D9` ring with 2px offset on focus.
  - AI Travel Omnibar: Multi-action floating input with heavy glass blur (`blur(20px)`), an embedded spark/action indicator, and micro-pills for active trip constraints (dates, budget, destination).

- **Cards:**
  - Base cards use `Level 1` glass styling with 16px border-radius, hairline borders, and generous interior padding (`space-lg`).
  - Destination media cards use edge-to-edge photography with a dark gradient scrim at the bottom (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.7)` to `transparent`), housing white typographic details.

- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 6px vertical by 12px horizontal padding.
  - AI metadata tags feature tinted translucent backgrounds (e.g., Rose Tint: `rgba(238, 211, 217, 0.12)` text `#EED3D9`; Sage Tint: `rgba(204, 211, 202, 0.2)` text `#A3ADA0`).

- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios):**
  - Crisp 18px boxes/circles with 1.5px `#B5C0D0` boundary. Checked state fills with `#EED3D9` and displays an animated checkmark.

- **Timeline Itinerary Component:**
  - Left-aligned persistent vertical track (`2px` width in `#B5C0D0`) linked with luminous circular state nodes. Active stops feature a rose pink ring with an interior pulse indicator.
